Automation

Tools I Made

Custom tools that remove repetitive design and drafting work.

LV SLD Builder - My last project

  • SLD
  • Excel
  • AI Assistant

Builds an LV single line diagram from a few inputs. Choose the transformer and RMU, paste your names from Excel, and the tool lays out the whole diagram.

  • Dynamic coffrets. Add or remove coffrets and the chain adjusts automatically.
  • Smart pillars. Add or remove pillars and the spacing updates itself.
  • Excel import. Paste coffret names for Loop 1 and Loop 2, and pillar link names, straight from Excel.
  • Transformer options. Switch between 1000 kVA and 1500 kVA.
  • RMU options. Choose 2+1 or 3+1.
  • Two pillar modes. Standard loop distribution, or a special mode that supplies a large building through two MDBs.
  • Custom outgoings. Add extra transformer outgoings and set breaker ratings in both AT and AF.
Built-in AI assistant

Upload an Excel sheet with your transformer, pillar and loop data. The assistant reads it and generates the full SLD configuration for you to review.

Pressure Pipes Converter

  • Civil 3D

Converts feature lines or polylines into Civil 3D pressure pipes automatically, with no need for Dynamo.

Wiring Tool

  • AutoCAD 2D

Draws wiring between blocks such as landscape lighting fixtures, street poles and smart poles. Select the blocks in order and the routine wires them automatically, using 45-degree arcs.

Circuit Numbering Tool

  • AutoCAD 2D

Numbers circuits automatically for landscape lighting fixtures, street poles and smart poles, in either single-phase or three-phase format.

Xref Polyline Extractor

  • AutoCAD 2D

Copies one selected polyline out of an xref. Only that polyline comes through, so there are no extra polylines to delete afterwards. It's also useful for drafting and wiring.
